01The flagship project

Meet DKAB360

DKAB360 is an AI-powered educational content automation platform designed to help educators and educational creators produce high-quality short-form learning content faster and more reliably.

Starting from a topic or learning objective, DKAB360 coordinates multiple stages of the production workflow, including research-assisted scripting, content refinement, scene planning, quality and safety checks, voice generation, visual orchestration, subtitles, rendering, and final review.

03A practical use for advanced AI

Built with AI at the Core

DKAB360 is being developed as an AI-native workflow where language models support reasoning, orchestration, coding, content refinement, and automated quality control.

We are exploring advanced AI systems such as Claude as reasoning and orchestration layers for agentic workflows, safer educational content generation, coding assistance, complex production decisions, and reliable quality assurance.

iThe platform is designed to integrate advanced language models; specific providers are being explored.
